About Nava Nalanda Mahavihara

His Excellency Dr. Rajendra Prasad, the first President of the Republic of India, initiated the idea of reviving the ancient seat of Buddhist learning at Nalanda. With this vision, the Government of Bihar established in 1951 a research institute for Pali and allied languages and Buddhist learning, which later came to be known as Nava Nalanda Mahavihara.

Today it functions as a Deemed-to-be University under the Ministry of Culture, Government of India, offering postgraduate, doctoral and certificate programmes across a range of classical and modern disciplines, and is ISO 21001:2018 certified.
